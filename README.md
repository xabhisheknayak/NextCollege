# NextCollege


Here is a comprehensive breakdown of all the features available on **NextCollege (Purnea College of Engineering)**:

---

### 🎨 1. Design & UI System
* **Neumorphic Soft UI Aesthetic**: Tactile 3D shadow system with light and dark mode toggles.
* **Responsive Layout**: Designed for seamless viewing across smartphones, tablets, and desktop displays.
* **Interactive Dynamic Animations**: Micro-interactions, smooth tab transitions, and subtle hover depth effects.

---

### 🎓 2. Student Dashboard
* **Dynamic Student ID Card**: Displays name, roll/registration number, branch code, semester, hostel status, and visual attendance ring percentage.
* **Subject-Wise Class Attendance Tracker**:
  * Checkboxes for each class in Today's Schedule: **Present**, **Absent**, or **Cancelled**.
  * Dynamic subject-wise percentage calculations (cancelled classes are automatically excluded from evaluation).
  * **Biometric Synchronization**: Integrates official biometric marks submitted directly by faculty.
  * **Attendance History Log**: View complete historical records of attended classes by date.
* **Find Classmates**:
  * Search peers and faculty by name, registration ID, or department.
  * View detailed profile modals with badges, branch info, and mutual connections.
  * Instant **Follow/Unfollow** and **Direct Messaging** options.
* **Campus Social Feed**:
  * Create posts with images and text announcements.
  * Like, comment, and engage with fellow students' posts.
* **Multi-Channel Chat System**:
  * **Branch Chat**: Dedicated chatrooms for specific engineering branches (e.g., CSE 105).
  * **Anonymous Forum**: Post questions and feedback anonymously with generated alias handles.
  * **Direct Messages**: One-on-one private messaging between students and professors.
* **Digital Library & Exam Archives**:
  * Digital access to textbooks, lab manuals, and lecture notes.
  * **Past Year Papers Archive**: Filterable repository for **Midsem Papers** and **Endsem Papers**.
* **Hostel Desk (for Hostellers)**:
  * **Digital Out-Pass Request**: Submit gate pass requests with real-time approval status tracking (Pending/Approved/Rejected).
  * **Warden Complaint System**: Log infrastructure or room maintenance issues directly to hostel wardens.
* **AI Study Copilot**:
  * Interactive AI academic assistant to answer subject queries.
  * Exam preparation guidance and custom study schedule generator.
* **Emergency SOS & Campus Navigator**:
  * **1-Click Emergency SOS**: Instant alert broadcast to campus security.
  * Nearest hospital locator & emergency hotline dispatch.
  * Interactive vector map of campus buildings, labs, and hostels.

---

### 👨‍🏫 3. Faculty / Professor Portal
* **Biometric Attendance Management**: Mark student attendance (Present/Absent/Cancelled) for scheduled lectures.
* **Class Schedule & Course Manager**: Overview of teaching timetables and subject allocations.
* **Hostel Warden Desk**: Wardens can review, approve, or reject student out-pass requests.
* **Notice & Assignment Announcements**: Broadcast official academic notices and deadlines directly to students.

---

### 🛡️ 4. Admin Control Center
* **Student Directory & Account Moderation**: Search, view academic records, update student details, or block/unblock accounts.
* **Faculty Directory Management**: Oversee professor profiles, assign warden privileges, and manage departments.
* **Campus Broadcast Noticeboard**: Publish and remove campus-wide official announcements.
* **Gate Security & Out-Pass Oversight**: Centralized log of all student out-passes for security verification.
* **System Health & Safety Dashboard**: Real-time overview of active platform users, hostel stats, and emergency alerts.
