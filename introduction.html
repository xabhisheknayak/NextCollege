Why This Platform Is Unique

Local-first & Private:

Demo data seeds into your browser's localStorage so the app works offline and keeps your data local by default.

Integrated AI Copilot:

Built-in study assistant gives quick answers, study plans and exam prep without leaving the dashboard.

All-in-one Campus Toolset:

Schedules, library, SOS, maps, chat and directory live together — no separate systems or logins required for demo use.

Lightweight & Self-hostable:

Static HTML/CSS/JS — easy to host on any static hosting (Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages) and fast to load.

Modular Demo Data:

`DEMO_DATA` provides easy examples you can extend; changes persist locally for quick prototyping.
